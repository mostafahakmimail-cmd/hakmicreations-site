What the AI Coach shares

Effective date: 17 November 2026

This page gives the details behind the short notice you see before using the Krateo AI Coach. The Coach is optional: it only works after you tap Agree & start, and you can switch it off at any time. It is part of our Privacy Policy, which explains everything else Krateo does with your data.

Who receives it

The Coach runs on Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, PBC (USA). When you use the Coach, Krateo sends the data below to Anthropic, through our own server, so Claude can write the answer. Anthropic acts as our service provider.

What is shared

Each time you send a message, ask for feedback on a workout or open your weekly review, Krateo shares:

  • your message and the earlier messages of the same chat,
  • the coach style you chose (direct or motivating),
  • for workout feedback, the workout you asked about,
  • a summary of your training.

The training summary contains:

  • your goal and target workouts per week,
  • how many workouts you did each week, and sessions missed,
  • your routines: their exercises, sets and rep ranges (so the Coach can suggest changes, which you approve before they apply),
  • for your most-trained exercises: sessions, best recent set, estimated one-rep max and volume,
  • recent personal records and your last workout,
  • working sets per muscle per week,
  • your latest body weight and how it changed (this can come from Apple Health),
  • if you added them under About you: your height, your age (worked out from your year of birth) and your sex. "Prefer not to say" is never sent.

When you ask the Coach to build a training split (a weekly plan), Krateo shares your answers to its questions (goal, level, days, session length, equipment, your notes) and, if you added them, your height, age and sex.

Training, body-weight, height, age and sex information counts as health data. By tapping Agree & start, you give your explicit consent to sharing it with Anthropic for this purpose.

What is never shared

Your name, email address, heart rate, water, supplements and photos are never sent to the Coach. Please don't type personal details, such as your name or medical conditions, into the chat.

Why

Only to write Coach answers that fit your training. The data is not used for advertising, and we don't sell it.

How long it is kept

  • On your phone: the chat is saved only on your phone. It is deleted when you clear the chat, sign out, delete your account, switch accounts or remove the app.
  • On our server: we don't keep your messages or the answers. We keep only how many messages you used and their size, to apply your plan's limits.
  • Reported answers: if you report a Coach answer, we keep that answer, the question before it and your account ID for 12 months so we can review it, then delete it automatically. It is deleted earlier if you delete your account.
  • At Anthropic: under Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default, and Anthropic deletes it from its systems within 30 days. Anthropic can keep it longer only where the law requires it, or, for up to 2 years, if a request is flagged as breaking its usage policy.

Transfer outside the EU

Anthropic is in the United States. The transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in Anthropic's Data Processing Addendum.

Changing your mind

  • Switch it off: Settings → AI Coach. Nothing more is sent from then on. Withdrawing doesn't affect what was sent before.
  • Changes: if what the Coach shares changes, the app asks for your agreement again before sending anything.
  • Your other rights (access, deletion, complaint to the CNIL) are explained in the Privacy Policy.
