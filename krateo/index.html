What Krateo does

Log every set, fast

Start from a routine or an empty workout. Weight, reps, rest timer and notes, with your last numbers ready beside each set.

200 exercises, pictured

Every exercise has its own 3D picture with the working muscles highlighted, plus how to do it.

Plans that fit your week

Answer a few questions and Krateo builds a routine split for your goal, schedule and equipment.

Progress you can read

Records, volume, sets per muscle and a muscle map, by week, month or year.

An AI coach that knows your training

Ask what to train today or why a lift is stuck. Answers use your own workouts. Training advice only, not medical advice.

Water and supplement reminders

Choose your goal and times. Tick them off from the notification or the home screen widget.

Apple Health and Apple Watch

Save workouts and water to Health, and follow your sets, rest and heart rate from your wrist.

Share your workout

Put your stats on your own photo in Instagram Story size, in five styles.
