Support

Krateo help

Write to hakmicreations@gmail.com. One person answers, usually within two working days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Krateo and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date or the end of a free trial. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email. Open the link in the email on your iPhone and set a new password.

How do I delete my account?

In Krateo go to Profile, open your account card and tap Delete account. This removes your account and all its data from our servers. It does not cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel that first.

What does Krateo do with Apple Health?

Only if you turn it on: it saves your workouts and water to Health, and reads your body weight and the heart rate recorded during workouts. Health data is never used for advertising and never sold.

What does the AI Coach see?

A summary of your training (goal, routines, recent workouts, records, body weight) and your question. Never your name, email, heart rate or photos. The full list is in the app and in the Privacy Policy.

The Coach gave a bad answer

Tap Report under the answer in the app, or email us the question and the answer.
