Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026

Krateo is a workout tracker for iPhone (and Apple Watch). It is made by Mostafa HAKMI HAKOUMI, an individual developer publishing under the brand name "Hakmi Creations", based in France ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the personal data described here.

Contact for anything about your data: hakmicreations@gmail.com Postal address: 147 Rue Vincent Raspail, 83130 La Garde, France

1. The short version

  • You need an account (email and password) to use Krateo. Your training data is stored on your phone and copied to our cloud server so it is backed up and synced.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites. Krateo has no analytics or advertising tools.
  • Apple Health, the AI Coach, notifications and photos are all optional and only work if you switch them on.
  • The AI Coach sends a summary of your training to Anthropic (the company that makes Claude) to write an answer. Your name and email are never sent.
  • You can export your data and delete your account from inside the app at any time.

2. What we collect and why

Your account

  • Email address and password. Used to create your account, sign you in and sync your data. Passwords are handled by our hosting provider, Supabase, and are stored only in hashed form; we never see them.
  • Account ID. A random identifier that links your data to your account.

Legal basis (GDPR): performance of our contract with you (providing the app).

Your training data (synced to the cloud)

When you are signed in, the app copies the following to our server so you can restore it and use it on another device:

  • workouts (exercises, sets, weights, reps, times, notes, dates),
  • routines and custom exercises you create,
  • body measurements you log (for example body weight),
  • your app settings (for example units, goals, reminders).

Legal basis: performance of our contract with you.

Some of this, such as body weight, can be health-related data. By logging it you choose to store it in Krateo; you can delete any entry, or your whole account, at any time.

Apple Health (optional)

Only if you switch it on in the app and allow it in Apple's permission screen:

  • Krateo reads: your latest body weight, the heart rate recorded during your workouts (for example by an Apple Watch), and workouts.
  • Krateo writes: your strength workouts (with estimated active energy) and the water you log.
  • Where it goes:

- Heart rate is only shown on your phone; it is not stored by us or sent anywhere. - Body weight read from Apple Health is added to your Krateo body log, so it is synced to our server like any weight you type in, and it can be included in the AI Coach summary if you use the Coach.

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties except as described in this policy to run the features you use. You can switch Health off in Krateo, and remove access at any time in the iPhone Settings app (Health → Data Access & Devices).

Legal basis: your explicit consent (Apple's permission screen and the switch in the app).

AI Coach (optional)

The Coach only works after you read and accept a consent screen the first time you open it.

When you send a message: - your goal and weekly goal, - your body weight trend, - your routines, - the exercises from about the last 8 weeks, with your best sets, estimated one-rep maxes and training volume, - your recent records, - your number of sets per muscle.

  • What is not sent: your name and email are never part of the summary. Please don't type personal details (such as your name or health conditions) into the chat.
  • The path it takes: the request goes to our server (a Supabase Edge Function), which checks that you are signed in and within your message limit, then sends it to Anthropic to generate the answer. The answer comes back the same way.
  • What we keep:

- We don't store your messages or the answers on our server. The conversation is saved only on your phone. - We do store, per account, how many Coach messages you used and how many AI "tokens" they used (to apply your plan's limits and control costs).

  • What Anthropic does with it: Anthropic processes the request as our service provider under its commercial terms.
  • Where it goes: Anthropic is based in the United States. See section 4 about transfers outside the EU.

The Coach gives general training suggestions. It is an AI: answers can be wrong, and it is not a doctor or a medical service.

Legal basis: your consent (the Coach consent screen). You can stop using the Coach at any time; deleting your account deletes your usage counts.

Photos and camera (optional)

If you use "Share on my photo", the app asks for access to your camera or photo library. The photo is used only on your phone to create the share image. Krateo does not upload or store your photos. If you then share the image, it goes wherever you choose (for example Instagram or WhatsApp), under that service's own privacy policy.

Notifications (optional)

Water and supplement reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. We don't use push notification servers and don't collect a notification token.

Importing and exporting

  • If you import a file from another workout app, it is read and converted on your phone; the result is then synced like any other workout.
  • When you export your data, the file is created on your phone and you choose where to send it.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, no data brokers.
  • No selling or renting of personal data.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites (Krateo does not use Apple's advertising identifier).

4. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers (Anthropic, Supabase as a company, RevenueCat) are based in the United States. When data is transferred there, we rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in the provider's data processing agreement.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and synced data: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Coach usage counts: for as long as your account exists; they are deleted with your account.
  • Coach conversations: only on your phone. They are deleted when you clear the chat, sign out, or remove the app. Anthropic keeps requests for a limited time as described in section 2.
  • Backups: deleted data can remain in our provider's encrypted backups for a short period before they are overwritten.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → account card → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and all your data on our server (workouts, routines, custom exercises, body entries, settings and Coach usage), then clears the data on that phone.

This does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel it in the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

You can also ask us to delete your account by email at hakmicreations@gmail.com.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • access your data and get a copy of it (also available in the app: Profile → Export),
  • correct it,
  • delete it,
  • restrict or object to its processing,
  • data portability,
  • withdraw consent at any time (Apple Health, Coach, camera and photos, notifications). This doesn't affect processing done before you withdrew it.

To use these rights, email hakmicreations@gmail.com. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL (www.cnil.fr), or to the authority in your EU country.

8. Children

Krateo is not intended for children. You must be at least 16 years old to create an account. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Each user can only read their own data (database row-level security).
  • The AI key is kept on our server, never in the app.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app before they apply.
